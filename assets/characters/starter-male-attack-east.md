# v1.5.9 — east-facing one-handed sword normal attack
Four weapon-free poses redrawn from the selected new overhead-slash concept, alpha-cleaned and normalized to 384x96 lossless WebP. Right-hand sockets and angles are estimated from the new poses. For accepted normal attacks targeting the east sector, male unmounted characters with one right-hand one-handed sword and empty left hand play 110/55/65/150 ms frames, scaled to the existing 380 ms cooldown (preview was 740 ms). Weapon uses existing generic sword art. Independent procedural slash crescent fades in/out from 110–330 ms, peak at frame 3; optional item slashColor (#RRGGBB) or element selects color, default blue. Existing hit VFX, damage and combat numbers remain immediate and unchanged; this is a visual-only prototype, not delayed damage or ASPD implementation. Moving while attacking retains movement and prioritizes attack art. Unsupported directions/equipment or missing asset fall back to legacy rendering. Mock Canvas tests cover timing, colors, angle, all eight existing movement directions, idle, asset fallback, pause and map changes. Actual iPad appearance and Pages deployment not verified.
