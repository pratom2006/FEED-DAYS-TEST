# v1.6.3 — southwest one-handed sword attack
The previous main v1.6.2 did not support southwest attack art. Southwest now uses the existing approved SE atlas, with the entire sprite, grip, sword and slash transformed together. No new image, no changes to gameplay, attack range or ASPD. Mirroring may change apparent anatomical hand while equipment remains right-hand. Mock Canvas tests: 30 mirrored time samples with identical SE output, 24 E/W regressions, 128 movement draws, idle, expiry, cancellation and missing asset fallback. The reported visual glitch has not been reproduced on a real device; Pages deployment and iPad appearance not verified.
